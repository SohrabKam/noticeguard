The problem

One missed deadline is all it costs

Under the Housing Grants, Construction and Regeneration Act 1996, if you miss the deadline to serve a Pay Less Notice, you owe the full amount the subcontractor claimed — regardless of whether the work was done.

⚠ The risk is real, not theoretical

If you're running JCT or NEC subcontracts across 10+ live projects, you're tracking deadlines in spreadsheets, juggling UK bank holidays that shift due dates, and hoping nothing slips. NoticeGuard automates the entire notice regime — deadlines, freeze-at-serve figures, email delivery, and audit trail — so you're never exposed by a missed date.
