```
Subject: [Company Name] — subcontract notice tracking

Hi [First Name],

I'm building a tool that helps commercial teams running JCT/NEC
subcontracts track payment notices and subbie drawdowns — catching
missed deadlines before they trigger a smash-and-grab.

We're looking for 5-6 pilot contractors at £250/month to shape the
product before wider release. No commitment beyond a 15-minute call
to see if it's relevant.

Open to a quick chat this week or next?

Sohrab
NoticeGuard
https://noticeguard.app
```

## Rules

1. **Send from sohrab@noticeguard.app** (set this up if not done)
2. **No LinkedIn** — email only, protects your day job profile
3. **Send 5-10 per day**, not a blast. Track responses in the CSV
4. **If no reply after 5 days**, send one follow-up:
   ```
   Subject: Re: [Company Name] — subcontract notice tracking
   
   Hi [First Name] — just bumping this. Happy to send a 2-min
   Loom walkthrough instead if that's easier.
   ```
5. **If they say no**, reply "Thanks, appreciate the response" and move on
6. **Do not offer free pilots** — £250/mo filters for serious users
